The autonomic nervous system regulates your fight-or-flight response. When stress becomes chronic, vagal tone decreases.

## The Role of Deep Somatic Exhalation

By extending your exhalations to double the length of your inhalations, you immediately signal to the brain stem that the threat has passed.

### Key Protocol Steps:
1. Inhale deeply for **4 seconds**.
2. Hold briefly for **2 seconds**.
3. Exhale smoothly for **8 seconds**.
